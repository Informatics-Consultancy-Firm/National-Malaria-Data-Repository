Use Case 17: Insecticide Resistance Data Management and Analysis National (NMCP) and District (Level 1). Manage and analyse insecticide resistance data by period. Provide a dedicated insecticide resistance module to import and standardise data, provide dynamic interactive analysis and enable data and report downloads by period. Insecticide resistance information is readily available for analysis, reporting and programme decision making.
