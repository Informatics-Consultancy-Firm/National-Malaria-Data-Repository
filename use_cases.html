About the NMDR Use Cases

Informatics Consultancy Firm Sierra Leone (ICF-SL) developed the Sierra Leone National Malaria Data Repository (NMDR) as a robust platform for decision making. With the support of the National Malaria Control Programme (NMCP), ICF-SL is now sharing the use cases that guided its setup, so that other countries can learn from them and build their own.

These use cases have delivered real impact. Because the system identifies issues as soon as the data arrive, teams can follow up and fix problems quickly, and this has greatly strengthened the health system. Moreover, the platform works on Android and iOS devices, which brings data to the doorstep of health staff wherever they work.

  • Finds issues earlyProblems in the data show up as soon as they arrive.
  • Fast follow upTeams know where to act and fix problems quickly.
  • Stronger health systemBetter data leads to better and faster decisions.
  • Android and iOSData reaches health staff on the phones they carry.
