Before you begin

Read the rules, enter your name and start when you are ready.

  • Ten case scenarios drawn from the questions Form C asks, each with four options.
  • Fifteen minutes on the clock. The assessment closes and marks itself when the time runs out.
  • The pass mark is ten out of ten. The certificate is issued only on a full score.
  • Answers are never shown, so read each scenario carefully before choosing.
  • Two attempts only, counted against your name and against this device.

Press Listen on any scenario to have the situation, the question and all four options read out, and Listen again to stop.
