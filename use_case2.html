Use Case 2: Achieving 100% Monthly Malaria Reporting for Key Malaria Variables National (NMCP), District (Level 1), Chiefdom (Level 2), PHU (Level 3) and Partners. Achieve complete monthly reporting of key malaria variables from every reporting facility and CHW. Automatically assess monthly reporting of OPD, suspected malaria, tested, confirmed and treated malaria; classify reporting as reported, complete or incomplete and support follow up until reporting is completed. Monthly reporting gaps are identified and followed up, supporting the programme goal of 100% reporting.

Source: NMDR Functional Use Cases, NMCP SM&E Unit, March 2026 (Table 2.0, Malaria Surveillance).

1.Objective

The NMDR should provide an automated mechanism for monitoring monthly malaria reporting by every health facility and Community Health Worker (CHW), identifying reporting gaps, and supporting follow up until all required reports are submitted.

2.Proposed NMDR Functionality

  1. Monitor the monthly reporting deadline

    Facilities and CHWs are expected to enter their monthly malaria data into HMIS (DHIS2) by the 25th of the following month. For example, July data should be entered by 25 August.

  2. Automatically pull data from HMIS

    The NMDR should use an automated pipeline to retrieve the key malaria data elements required for reporting from HMIS (DHIS2) after the reporting deadline.

  3. Determine whether a facility or CHW has reported

    The system should assess each facility and CHW for the reporting month. If at least one of the defined key malaria variables has been reported, the facility or CHW should be classified as reported. If none have been reported, it should be classified as non reporting.

  4. Assess completeness of the reported malaria data

    For facilities and CHWs identified as having reported, the NMDR should assess whether all five key malaria data elements have been reported.

  5. Generate reporting gap lists

    The NMDR should automatically generate lists of non reporting facilities and CHWs, incomplete facilities and CHWs, and facilities and CHWs with complete reporting.

  6. Support investigation of reporting gaps

    For non reporting or incomplete facilities and CHWs, the NMDR should support follow up to determine the source of the gap. Where necessary, facilities should be requested to provide their monthly summary forms to establish whether the data were not captured, captured but not entered into DHIS2, or not entered correctly at the chiefdom or district level.

  7. Track follow up and resolution

    The NMDR should allow reporting gaps to be followed until the missing information is submitted. Once updated data are available in HMIS, the system should automatically reassess the facility or CHW reporting status.

  8. Provide monthly reporting monitoring outputs

    The NMDR should provide dashboards, tables, heatmaps, maps and downloadable lists showing monthly reporting performance and outstanding reporting gaps at facility, CHW, chiefdom, district and national levels.

3.Key Malaria Data Elements

The NMDR should assess the following five key malaria data elements when determining whether a monthly report is complete.

1OPD
2Suspected malaria
3Tested
4Confirmed
5Treated

4.Reporting Classification

ClassificationDefinition
Non reporting None of the five key malaria data elements has been reported for the month.
Reported At least one of the five key malaria data elements has been reported for the month.
Incomplete reporting The facility or CHW has reported, but one or more of the five key malaria data elements is missing.
Complete reporting All five key malaria data elements have been reported for the month.

5.Intended Outcome

The NMDR should enable NMCP to move from waiting for reports and manually identifying missing submissions to a proactive system that continuously identifies who has not reported, supports follow up, and tracks outstanding gaps until they are resolved.

6.Overall Programme Goal

Programme goalTo achieve 100% monthly reporting from every health facility and every CHW, with all five key malaria data elements completed.

How it works in the NMDR

Step 1

Select the period

Choose the Start Month and End Month, then click Run Pipeline. The Methodology and Data Elements panel under the dates shows which facilities and data elements the run will use.

Step 2

Data fetching and automated analytical pipelines

The pipeline runs by itself and shows its progress stage by stage. It fetches the facility registry from DHIS2, pulls the data elements for every month in the period, determines the reporting status of each facility, computes the aggregates, then loads the maps and computes the geographic rates.

Step 3

Output

The live output opens directly below. Select the period and click Run Pipeline to see dashboards, tables, heatmaps, maps and downloadable lists of reporting performance and outstanding gaps at facility, CHW, chiefdom, district and national levels.
